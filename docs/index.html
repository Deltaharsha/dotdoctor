A checkup for your
Linux desktop.

dotdoctor scans your Hyprland setup and dotfiles for keybinds that fight each other, shortcuts that launch programs you don't have, broken symlinks and config errors. Then it tells you exactly how to fix them.

$ git clone https://github.com/Deltaharsha/dotdoctor && ./dotdoctor/dotdoctor.py

Free and open source. Works with Lua and classic Hyprland configs, including Omarchy.

What it checks

⌨️ Keybind conflicts

Reads your live binds from Hyprland, so it sees what's actually active, not what you think is.

👻 Missing programs

Shortcuts pointing at apps you uninstalled or never had.

🔗 Broken symlinks

Leftovers from an old dotfiles repo or a stow gone wrong.

🧹 Config clutter

Piles of .bak files and errors Hyprland is quietly logging.

FAQ

Does it change my files?

No. The free version only reads your config and prints suggestions. Pro fixes are opt-in and always back up first.

Which setups does it support?

Hyprland today (including Omarchy's Lua config). Sway, i3, Waybar and Neovim checks are on the roadmap. Tell us what you want next when you join the waitlist.

Does my config get uploaded anywhere?

The free version runs 100% locally. Pro's AI explain only sends the specific finding you ask about, never your whole config.
